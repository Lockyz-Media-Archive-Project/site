About This Game

This game is a tribute to early 2000s Flash and arcade-style gameplay. Designed with love and care, it captures the feel of web games while adding modern touches like controller support and save states.

Development started as a personal project but quickly evolved into something bigger. The goal was to preserve not just the gameplay but the culture and aesthetic of early internet fun.

Whether you're playing for nostalgia or discovering it for the first time, we hope you enjoy this experience.
